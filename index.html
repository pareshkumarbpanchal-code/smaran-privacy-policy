SMARAN — PRIVACY POLICY Version: 1.1 Effective Date: 27 September 2026 Smaran (“Smaran”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) is a personal memory collection and organisation application. This Privacy Policy explains how Smaran handles information when you use the Smaran Android application. We have written this policy to explain, in reasonably clear language, what information is processed, why it is processed, where it may be stored, and what choices you have. 1. Who This Policy Applies To This Privacy Policy applies to information processed through the Smaran Android application. It does not govern the independent privacy practices of third-party services such as Google Firebase, Google Drive, Google Gemini, Google Maps or other services that have their own privacy policies. 2. Information We Process Depending on the features you use, Smaran may process the following categories of information. 2.1 Account and Authentication Information Smaran uses phone-based authentication. This may involve: - your mobile phone number; - authentication information associated with your Firebase account; - authentication identifiers such as your Firebase user identifier; and - information required by Firebase Authentication to complete the authentication process. The phone number is used primarily to authenticate your Smaran account. 2.2 Profile Information Depending on the features you use, Smaran may process information such as: - your name; - companion or person names; - categories; - titles; and - other information you voluntarily enter. 2.3 Memory and User Content Smaran is specifically designed to store personal memories. You may voluntarily provide: - photographs; - videos; - audio recordings; - PDF documents; - journal entries; - notes; - memory titles; - activity information; - categories; - tags; - names of companions or other people; - location names; - dates; and - other memory-related information. This information may be highly personal. You should only upload information that you are legally entitled to store and process. 2.4 Location Information Smaran may use precise device location information when you use location-based features. Location may be used for purposes such as: - journey mapping; - identifying or displaying locations; - associating memories with locations; and - reverse geocoding. Location access is controlled through Android permissions. If you do not grant location permission, location-dependent features may not function. 2.5 Local Device Data Smaran stores application data locally on your Android device. This may include: - memories; - journal entries; - metadata; - AI-generated summaries or reflections; - locally stored media; - settings; - authentication/session information; and - other information required for the App to function. Smaran uses Android protected preference storage, including EncryptedSharedPreferences, for selected sensitive local values such as authentication/session information and the user-provided Gemini API key. The current Smaran Room database itself is not encrypted at the database level. Therefore, although Android application sandboxing and device security provide protection, Smaran does not claim that all locally stored data is encrypted. We strongly recommend using a secure Android device lock and keeping your device software up to date. 3. How We Use Information Smaran processes information for purposes including: Account Authentication To authenticate you and allow you to access your Smaran account. Memory Management To create, store, organise, display and search the memories you create. Media Management To store and display photographs, videos, audio recordings and documents associated with your memories. Location Features To provide journey mapping, location display and related location functionality. AI Features If you voluntarily enable AI functionality and provide your own Gemini API key, Smaran may send relevant textual memory information directly to Google's Gemini services for AI processing. The current implementation sends text information only, such as: - memory titles; - categories; - location names; - companion names; - journal entries; - notes; and - related textual memory information. Smaran does not currently send photographs, videos or audio recordings to Gemini for these AI features. AI responses may be stored in your local Smaran database and may subsequently be included in your Google Drive Vault backup if Vault synchronisation is enabled. Cloud Backup If you voluntarily connect Google Drive and use the Smaran Vault feature, Smaran may upload selected Smaran data to your own Google Drive account. 4. Google Drive Vault Smaran provides an optional Google Drive-based Vault. To use this feature, you must explicitly connect a Google Account. Smaran requests the Google Drive DRIVE_FILE scope. This scope is designed to allow Smaran to create, access, modify and delete files created by Smaran rather than granting Smaran general access to unrelated files in your Google Drive. Smaran may create a folder called: Smaran Archive and organise content within that folder. Depending on your use of Smaran, Vault may contain: - photographs; - videos; - audio recordings; - PDF documents; - memory-related metadata; - folder names; - memory titles; - categories; - companion/person names; and - AI-generated text stored as part of your memories or logs. 4.1 When Data Is Uploaded Google Drive synchronisation may occur: - when you manually select “Sync Now”; or - automatically after creating a new memory if Auto-Sync is enabled. 4.2 Security of Google Drive Data Data transmitted to Google Drive is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections. Smaran does not currently apply an additional application-level encryption layer to files before uploading them to Google Drive. Once stored in Google Drive, the information is subject to Google's security infrastructure and applicable Google policies. 4.3 Google Drive Deletion Deleting a memory from Smaran currently removes the local memory record but does not automatically delete its corresponding Google Drive backup. Disconnecting Google Drive also does not automatically delete previously uploaded Google Drive data. Smaran currently does not provide an in-app function to delete the entire “Smaran Archive” folder. If you want to remove cloud backup data, you must manage and delete it through your Google Drive account. 5. Google Gemini AI AI functionality is optional. To use the current Gemini-powered features, you must provide your own Google Gemini API key. The key is stored locally using protected preference storage. Smaran does not intentionally transmit your Gemini API key to a Smaran-operated server. When AI functionality is used, relevant textual information is transmitted directly to Google's Gemini service using the API key supplied by you. Smaran currently does not send photographs, videos or audio recordings to Gemini for the described AI functions. Because Gemini is a third-party service, information processed by Gemini is also subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy practices. You should review Google's current terms and privacy information before using AI features with sensitive information. 6. Google Maps and Geocoding Smaran may use Google Maps and Google geocoding functionality to provide location-related features. Depending on the feature being used, location coordinates or location names may be transmitted to Google's services to obtain mapping or geocoding information. These services are subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy practices. 7. Third-Party Services Smaran currently relies on third-party technologies and services that may process information necessary to provide functionality. These include: Firebase Authentication Used for phone-based authentication. Google Drive API Used for optional Smaran Vault backup. Google Gemini Used for optional AI functionality when the user provides and uses their own API key. Google Maps / Google Geocoding Used for location and journey-related functionality. Other technical libraries may process information locally or as necessary to provide application functionality. We do not sell your personal information. We do not operate a separate Smaran-owned cloud database for storing your memories. 8. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect for Advertising Smaran is not designed to sell personal information or use your memories, photographs, videos, recordings or documents for advertising purposes. We do not intentionally sell your personal information to data brokers. 9. Sharing of Information Smaran may transmit information to third-party service providers when necessary to provide a feature that you use. Depending on your use, this may include: - Firebase/Google for authentication; - Google Drive for Vault backup; - Google Gemini for optional AI functionality; - Google Maps/geocoding services for location functionality. Smaran does not intentionally share your personal memory content with unrelated advertisers or data brokers. We may also disclose information where required by applicable law, legal process, court order, or a valid governmental request. 10. Your Control Over Optional Features You control whether you use certain optional features. For example: - you can choose whether to connect Google Drive Vault; - you can disable Auto-Sync; - you can choose whether to use Gemini AI features; - you can choose whether to provide a Gemini API key; - you can control Android location permissions; and - you can choose what memories and media you upload. 11. Data Retention Smaran does not operate a general-purpose Smaran cloud database containing all of your memories. Information may remain: - on your Android device until you delete it or uninstall the App; - in your Google Drive until you delete the relevant cloud files; and - with third-party service providers for periods governed by their applicable policies and technical requirements. Deleting the Smaran application removes its local application data according to normal Android uninstall behavior. Uninstalling Smaran does not delete information previously uploaded to your Google Drive. 12. Deletion of Data You can delete individual memories from the App. However, because Smaran's current Google Drive implementation does not automatically delete corresponding cloud backups when a local memory is deleted, you may need to separately delete the relevant files from your Google Drive. Disconnecting Google Drive does not automatically delete existing cloud backups. If you wish to request deletion of information held by or processed by Smaran, you may use the Account Deletion functionality provided within the App or contact the Developer / Data Fiduciary / Operator using the contact information provided in Section 20. Where information is held directly by a third-party service such as Google, you may also need to exercise applicable rights directly with that service. 13. Account Deletion Smaran provides an Account Deletion option within the application. Users can initiate deletion of their Smaran account through the account-deletion functionality provided in the App. When an account deletion request is confirmed, Smaran will delete or permanently remove the account information and locally managed data associated with the account, subject to applicable technical and legal requirements. Because Smaran uses third-party services such as Google Firebase and Google Drive, deletion of information held directly by those services may require separate processing or may be subject to the applicable policies and retention requirements of those services. In particular: - Deleting a Smaran account does not automatically guarantee deletion of files previously uploaded to the user's Google Drive. - Google Drive backup data may need to be separately deleted from the user's Google Drive account. - Information that must be retained to comply with legal obligations may be retained for the period required by applicable law. Users may also contact the Developer / Data Fiduciary / Operator using the contact information in Section 20 for questions regarding account or data deletion. 14. Children's Privacy Smaran is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly design the service to collect personal information from children without appropriate legal authority or consent. If you believe that personal information belonging to a child has been submitted to Smaran improperly, please contact us. 15. Data Security We use reasonable technical and organisational measures appropriate to the design and use of Smaran. These include measures such as: - Android application sandboxing; - encrypted HTTPS transmission for network communications; - EncryptedSharedPreferences for selected sensitive local values; - protected authentication mechanisms; - restricted Google Drive OAuth scope; and - access controls provided by the underlying Android and Google services. However, no electronic system is completely secure. The Smaran local Room database is currently not encrypted at the database level. Users should therefore use a secure device lock, avoid sharing unlocked devices, and keep their Android operating system updated. 16. International Processing Some third-party services used by Smaran are operated by Google or other providers that may process information in countries outside your country of residence. Such processing is subject to the applicable terms, safeguards and privacy practices of those providers and applicable law. 17.
